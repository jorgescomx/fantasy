📖 How to find SWID & espn_s2 in 60 seconds
  1. Log in to fantasy.espn.com in your browser.
  2. Right-click anywhere and select Inspect (or press F12), then go to the Application (or Storage) tab.
  3. In the left sidebar, expand Cookies → click https://fantasy.espn.com.
  4. Find the row for SWID and copy its value (including the curly braces).
  5. Find the row for espn_s2 and copy its entire string value.
